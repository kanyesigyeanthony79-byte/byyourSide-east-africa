Frequently Asked Questions

ByYourSide adds personalised support to your existing safari plans—we do not replace your tour operator or guide.

Are you a tour operator or safari guide?

No. We are a travel companionship and personal assistance service. Your safari operator handles the itinerary, transport, accommodation, and guiding; we support you throughout the experience.

Can I book ByYourSide with any safari company?

Yes. We can work alongside your chosen operator, lodge, travel planner, or medical provider. Share your itinerary with us and we will coordinate the support you need.

What does a companion do during my safari?

Your companion can assist with airport arrival, hotel check-in, daily routines, mobility, communication, practical tasks, and reassurance—so you can enjoy the safari at your own pace.

Do you provide medical care or emergency services?

Our general travel companions provide non-clinical support only. For our Nurse-Accompanied Medical Travel service specifically, a qualified nurse travels with you, offering professional support, reassurance, and care coordination throughout the journey. In both cases, we work alongside — not in place of — your treating physicians and approved care team, and we do not provide emergency medical treatment.

Who is this service designed for?

We support international visitors who want extra confidence and independence, including older travellers, solo travellers, people with mobility needs, and guests whose family cannot travel with them.

Can families travelling with young children use ByYourSide?

Absolutely. We provide practical, non-clinical assistance during airport transfers, hotel stays, daily routines, family activities, and excursions. A parent or legal guardian remains responsible for the child at all times.

How do I arrange support?

Contact us before you travel with your dates, destinations, safari itinerary, and support needs. We will discuss the right companion plan and coordinate with your travel team.

Can ByYourSide accompany me outside East Africa?

Yes — this is one of our two outbound services. If your journey continues beyond East Africa, your companion can travel on with you. Each journey is assessed around the itinerary, destination requirements, companion availability, visas, travel arrangements, and applicable local regulations.
